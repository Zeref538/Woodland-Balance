# Woodland Balance

A local two-player 2D balance game based on John's sketch: two woodland scouts share a wooden plank resting over a large enchanted stone ball. Keep the boy black-haired and the girl auburn-haired.

Players move and jump independently, changing the plank balance through physics. Falling restarts the round; R restarts manually. No scoring or online play is required. Deliver the complete Unity project in its own repository, with artwork separately downloadable.
